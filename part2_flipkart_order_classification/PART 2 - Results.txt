CONFUSION ANALYSIS
==================

1. Shirt -> T-shirt/top

The model most frequently confused Shirt images with T-shirt/top images.
This is plausible because both categories represent upper-body clothing
and can have similar shapes and silhouettes in the small grayscale
Fashion-MNIST images. Details such as collars, sleeves and shirt
structure can be difficult to distinguish.


2. Shirt -> Coat

The second major confusion was between Shirt and Coat. These categories
can have overlapping upper-body silhouettes, especially when the original
low-resolution grayscale image does not clearly show clothing details.
The model therefore sometimes identifies the overall shape as a Coat
even when the actual class is Shirt.
